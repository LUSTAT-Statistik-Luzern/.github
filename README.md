# 👋 Welcome to the open source home of LUSTAT Statistik Luzern

**The independent competence centre for official statistics in the Canton of Lucerne.**

We are dedicated to providing politicians, businesses, and the public with independent, evidence-based information. This organization hosts the code and tools we use to analyze demographics, health, economy, and society in Lucerne.

## 🏛️ What We Do
*   **Official Statistics:** We collect and publish reliable data on the state of the canton.
*   **Open Government Data (OGD):** We serve as the OGD competence center for Lucerne, promoting free access to administrative data.
*   **Transparency:** We share our methods here to ensure our work is reproducible and trustworthy.

## 👩‍💻 Our Code
We use modern data science tools to build automated and reproducible statistical pipelines.
*   **Languages:** Primarily `R` and `Python`.
*   **Focus:** Data cleaning, statistical modeling, and automated reporting.
*   **Collaboration:** We follow the *Charter of Swiss Public Statistics* to ensure quality and neutrality.
